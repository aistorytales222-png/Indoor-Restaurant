About us

Indoor Restaurant is a family-friendly garden restaurant on Baadreni Road in Sauraha, serving Nepali and Tharu food.

Come for duck choila, ghungi, mutton bhutuwa, dal bhat and our Nepali khana set. Sit indoors, or outside in the garden in a private hut with its own table.
